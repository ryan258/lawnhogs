Most lawn care companies consider a job complete when they run out of gas. We operate under a rigid 10-commandment ethical and geometric doctrine.

THE 10 COMMANDMENTS OF TURF DOMINANCE

  1. I. THOU SHALT HONOR THE STRIPE

    A crooked line is a public declaration of spiritual defeat. All passes shall be drawn with laser or celestial alignment.

  2. II. THOU SHALT NEVER TEAR A BLADE OF GRASS

    Blades are sharpened every 8 operating hours. Torn grass tips turn brown. Brown is the color of weakness.

  3. III. THOU SHALT TRENCH A 90-DEGREE EDGE

    The boundary between sidewalk concrete and Kentucky bluegrass is absolute. No creeping fescue shall violate the pavement.

  4. IV. THOU SHALT LEAVE ZERO CLIPPINGS IN THE DRIVEWAY

    Post-cut air velocity shall exceed 200 MPH until every stray clipping is returned to the turf or incinerated in the mulching chamber.

  5. V. THOU SHALT RESPECT THE BACKYARD GATE

    If a gate is latched upon entry, it shall be latched upon departure. Golden Retrievers are precious; loose dogs are a logistical failure.

  6. VI. THOU SHALT NEVER MOW IN RAIN-SOAKED MUD

    We possess weather radar and windows. Ruts in the yard are unforgivable sins.

  7. VII. THOU SHALT OUTCLASS STEVE ACROSS THE STREET

    The primary metric of success is the visible distress of neighbors with inferior equipment.

  8. VIII. THOU SHALT WEAR THE TRUCKER HAT WITH PRIDE

    Uniformity creates authority. An unkempt operator cannot demand order from an unkempt lawn.

  9. IX. THOU SHALT COMMUNICATE DIRECTLY

    No corporate buzzwords. If the lawn needs lime, we tell you it needs lime. If Steve's tree branches are encroaching, we alert the watchtower.

  10. X. FULL SEND. NO BS.

    We leave when the yard looks like a high-definition video game simulation. Not one second prior.
