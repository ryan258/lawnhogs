THE 10 COMMANDMENTS OF TURF DOMINANCE

  1. 01

    HONOR THE STRIPE

    A crooked line is a public declaration of spiritual defeat. All passes are drawn with laser or celestial alignment.

  2. 02

    SHARP BLADES ONLY

    Blades are sharpened every 8 operating hours. Torn grass tips turn brown. Brown is the color of weakness.

  3. 03

    CRISP 90° EDGES

    The boundary between concrete and bluegrass is absolute. No creeping fescue shall violate the pavement.

  4. 04

    NO CLIPPINGS LEFT BEHIND

    Post-cut air velocity exceeds 200 MPH until every stray clipping is returned to the turf or mulched.

  5. 05

    RESPECT THE GATE

    Latched on entry means latched on departure. Golden Retrievers are precious; loose dogs are a logistical failure.

  6. 06

    NO MOWING IN MUD

    We possess weather radar and windows. Ruts in the yard are unforgivable sins.

  7. 07

    OUTCLASS THE NEIGHBORS

    The primary metric of success is the visible distress of Steve across the street and his inferior equipment.

  8. 08

    SHOW UP LOOKING PROFESSIONAL

    Uniformity creates authority. An unkempt operator cannot demand order from an unkempt lawn.

  9. 09

    COMMUNICATE DIRECTLY

    No corporate buzzwords. If the lawn needs lime, we tell you it needs lime. No runaround, ever.

  10. 10

    FULL SEND. NO BS.

    We leave when the yard looks like a high-definition video game simulation. Not one second prior.
