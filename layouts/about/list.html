THE LAWN HOGS MANIFESTO

In 2018, our founder Johnny Hogger looked across an ordinary suburban street and saw a landscape of tragedy. Uneven lawn clippings piled in heaps. Sidewalk edges looking like jagged saw blades. High-school teenagers pushing rusted 21-inch mowers while listening to podcasts and texting their friends.

He realized the entire lawn care industry was suffering from a critical lack of swagger, horsepower, and geometric integrity.
