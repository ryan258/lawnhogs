To the untrained eye, lawn striping looks like lawn mowing. To a certified Lawn Hog, it is the manipulation of solar photon reflection against biological chlorophyll planes.

### How Lawn Stripes Actually Work
Grass blades do not change color when they are striped. Rather, the roller trailing behind the cutting deck physically bends the blade of grass either toward the viewer or away from the viewer:

* **Bending Away From Viewer:** Light reflects off the broad, shiny waxy surface of the blade, appearing as a **brilliant light green**.
* **Bending Toward Viewer:** You look into the shadowed underside and tips of the blades, appearing as a **deep, saturated dark green**.

### The 45-Degree Compass Advantage
Why do we cut at 45-degree angles relative to your front door?

When you step out of your house in the morning, the solar azimuth angle strikes the turf at a low angle. Cutting parallel or perpendicular to the street wastes 60% of potential photon reflection. Cutting at 45 degrees ensures that regardless of whether the viewer is approaching from the east or west cul-de-sac, the stripes appear three-dimensional.

### The Psychological Effect on Observers
When a passerby looks at an unstriped lawn, their brain processes it as "ambient yard." When they encounter precision diamond stripes, the brain recognizes intentional human mastery over nature. It signals discipline, wealth, and dangerous levels of free time.
