The cut isn't finished when the blades shut off. It is finished when the driveway looks like a freshly swept tennis court.

### The Problem with Amateur Cleanups
Most lawn crews hold a tiny handheld electric blower with one hand while walking backwards to their truck, leaving trails of dried clippings in the driveway gutters, blowing grass into fresh mulch, and spraying dust onto parked cars.

### The Cyclone Standard
Every Lawn Hogs dispatch culminates in a high-velocity air purification sweep using commercial 75.6cc 4-stroke backpack blowers outputting over 1,000 CFM at 215 MPH.

1. **Mulch Protection:** We blow outward from beds toward the lawn, ensuring no mulch is displaced.
2. **Driveway & Walkway Clearing:** Every trace of green organic matter is returned to the turf.
3. **Porch & Step Detail:** Front entrance and back patio spaces are swept spotlessly clean.
