The zero-turn mower handles the open expanses; the string trimmer handles the tactical close-quarters combat.

### Precision Around Every Obstacle
Amateur landscapers scalp the grass down to the dirt around fence posts and tree trunks, creating unsightly "crop circles" of dead soil. At Lawn Hogs, our technicians match the exact height of the zero-turn deck on every single border pass.

### What We Clear:
* **Mailbox Outposts:** No shaggy tufts surrounding your post.
* **Ornamental Garden Beds:** Crisp transitions where mulch meets sod.
* **AC Unit & Utility Enclosures:** Zero overgrowth choking your condenser coils.
* **Fencing Perimeters:** Clean, uniform borders along wood, vinyl, and chainlink fences.
