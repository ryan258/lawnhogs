Lawn dominance is a year-round campaign. When the seasons shift, turf faces unique existential threats: soil compaction, leaf suffocation, and subterranean thatch choking.

### Spring Awakening Package
* **Mechanical Power De-thatching:** Extracting built-up dead organic matter so sunlight and water can penetrate to the root zone.
* **Core Plug Aeration:** Pulling thousands of 3-inch soil cores to alleviate suburban clay compaction and facilitate deep root propagation.

### Autumn Leaf Cleansing Package
* **Heavy Leaf Ingestion & Bagging:** Eradicating wet, heavy oak and maple leaves before mold can take hold of your cool-season turf.
* **Winterizer Mineral Balance:** Applying high-potassium, slow-release nitrogen fertilizers to fortify root crowns through freezing winters.
